## 2024-05-18 - Aggregate success metrics for empty states
**Learning:** Aggregate statistics across multiple operations provide a stronger sense of accomplishment and immediate value compared to isolated per-item statistics, even when the state is empty (e.g. 0 items updated). Hiding these metrics removes closure.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to aggregate success metrics (like total space freed or total items updated) at the end of batch operations, and ensure they are displayed even when the total is zero.
