## 2024-05-24 - Empty State Semantics
**Learning:** Structural empty states (like "no containers") should use the fast-forward emoji (⏩️) to imply intentional and efficient processing, rather than the skip-track emoji (⏭️). The skip-track emoji should be reserved for items that are explicitly excluded by the user.
**Action:** Use ⏩️ for structural empty states in logging and remote payloads, and keep ⏭️ only for explicit exclusions.
